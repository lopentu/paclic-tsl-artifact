# Robustness re-analyses of the existing data

Model outputs: `qwen3vl_8b`, clip kind `combined`. 55 events; Tier-2 gate = mean rating >= 2. Generated by `scripts/18_analyze_robustness_checks.py`; no inference and no new human rating is involved, so every number here is recomputed from files already in the repository.

Every statistic here, agreement included, is computed on `events_checked.csv`, which holds the two annotators' final scores and is the annotation of record for this study. An earlier pass exists in which the annotators were still applying subtly different readings of the rubric; its disagreement measures that drift rather than any property of the construct or of the items, so it is not a reliability estimate for this reference. It is neither released nor used anywhere below.

## 9. How much of the Tier-2 collapse is the rubric's own cap? 

Appendix A's Step 1 caps a row at <= 1 when the model's self-reported articulator does not match the specified one. In the articulator-withheld cells the model was asked to choose it, so 'the collapse is mediated by picking the wrong articulator' is partly true by construction: a mismatch forces an inadequate rating regardless of how the description reads. Below: how many rows the cap can reach, and what the withheld-cell adequacy rate is on the cap-unreachable rows alone -- an upper bound on what the cue could be doing beyond redirection.

This does NOT recover uncapped scores. Reporting the description quality of a mismatched row independently of which articulator it describes needs a rater to re-score those rows with Step 1 suspended; it is the one check in this file that cannot be done from existing files.

## 10. Translation-driven lexical appeal, counted

The paper illustrates translation-driven distortion with one item (C15) and declines to quantify it. A description that names a lexical sign -- a glossed word in quotes, `the sign for X`, `signing X` -- is the observable trace of reasoning from the supplied translation to an expected form rather than reporting visible form. Counting that pattern per cell makes the direction testable: it should be commoner where the translation is supplied.

| `ablate_translation` | no | 7/55 | 0.127 |
