## 1. Is the J(X, A) > J(X, B) ordering a length artifact?

`B` is a short dedicated field and `X` a full structured description, so Jaccard's union denominator caps J(X, B) at |B|/|X| even under total containment. Containment divides by the smaller set instead and has no such ceiling. The permutation null pairs each X with ANOTHER event's B, which holds the field's format and register constant and varies only whose non-dominant hand it describes.

## 6. How many frames does the shuffled condition actually shuffle?

Both the target clip and the context clip are degraded and both are sent, so the shuffled input is the sum of the two. Frames consumed = clip duration times the configured sampling rate.

- sampling rate: 16 fps
- padded target clip: mean 1.13 s (range 0.72-2.60) -> mean 18 frames (range 12-42)
- context window clip: mean 5.26 s (range 2.60-11.91) -> mean 84 frames (range 42-191)
- both clips together: mean 102 frames per item
