Answers: {'right': 37, 'left': 3}. Against the expert labels: **37/40 correct**. Expert label distribution: {'right': 40}, so always answering `right` scores 40/40 = 1.000 -- **the model does not beat that constant baseline, so this accuracy is not evidence of a handedness representation.** With every label `right` there is no label variance to discriminate on, so accuracy here cannot separate 'perceives handedness' from 'always answers the majority hand'.
